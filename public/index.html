Partial fill-in or full removal

California cities allow two methods, and the choice decides what can be built on the land afterwards. A partial fill-in breaks holes through the bottom of the shell, removes the top of the wall and fills the rest with soil. The Los Angeles backfill bulletin P/BC 2026-111 allows that only for landscaping use on ground sloping 10% or less. Long Beach goes further: its bulletin IB-008 requires a recorded covenant on the property when a shell stays.

A full removal hauls away the whole shell and replaces it with fill compacted to 90% and certified by an engineer. Stockton's form BLDG-0014 sets the two routes side by side as Option A and Option B, the clearest statement of the choice in any city document.

What pool removal costs in California

California contractors publish higher prices than the national cost guides. Northern California firms quote $3,500 to $12,000 for a partial fill-in and $8,000 to $30,000 for full removal, according to published price lists from Souza & Son's, Bye Bye Pool and Walberg, Inc. Los Angeles and Orange County firms start higher: Los Angeles Pool Demolition Contractors lists prices from $10,500 for a partial and $17,500 for a full removal. The pool removal cost guide lists every published price with its source.

Comparing written quotes from licensed contractors is the surest way to price a specific pool.

Licence checks behind each listing

Listings come from each firm's own website, or its Google Maps listing where it has no site. Of the 103 businesses, 54 show a California licence number, and the state board check found 51 of those current and active and 3 not active. Businesses with an inactive licence are held back. Another 33 show no number and go on a page only after a phone check, as do the 6 that exist only as Google Maps listings. Ten lead-generation sites that pass enquiries to unnamed contractors are left off entirely.

An active licence means the Contractors State License Board lookup shows the licence as current and active on the date checked. The board's record also lists the licence classes and the workers' compensation policy, both worth a look before signing a contract. The listing criteria give the full method.

Common questions from homeowners

Does pool removal need a permit in California?
Yes in every city whose handout was read: a grading permit in Los Angeles and Long Beach, a demolition permit in San Diego, Riverside and Fontana, and a building permit in San Jose, Irvine and Stockton.

Can a house or ADU be built over a filled pool?
Only where the shell was removed and the fill compacted to 90% and certified by an engineer, as the Fontana handout states directly.

How long does pool removal take?
One to four days for a partial fill-in and three to seven for a full removal, with permits adding one to three weeks in most cities.

Is there a rebate for removing a pool?
No statewide programme exists. Valley Water in Santa Clara County pays $2 a square foot, up to $3,000, when a pool becomes low-water landscape.
