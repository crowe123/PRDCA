Who may pull each Stockton option

Option A permits go to an owner-builder with a completed form or a contractor holding an A, C-12 or C-53 licence. Option B needs a California-licensed contractor, a geotechnical report from a licensed engineer, a special inspection and testing agreement and a final compaction report. Either way, gas, water and sewer lines are capped and all electrical pool equipment, including the breaker, is removed.

The job has at least two inspections:

  1. An in-progress inspection before the pool is filled, covering the holes or the shell removal and the erosion control.
  2. A final inspection once backfill is complete, with signed compaction reports on site.

Partial fill-in or full removal in Stockton

Option A is for open space or landscaping that will not support any structure, and the shell may stay. At least four holes, each at least 1 ft across and 2 ft deep, go through the bottom with one at the deepest point, at least 12 inches of crushed rock covers the bottom, and the top 2 ft of shell below finished grade is demolished, with the top 24 inches filled with organic soil fit for planting. Option B applies whenever the area will support a structure, including a patio cover or deck: the shell is removed entirely and replaced with engineered fill under a geotechnical engineer's supervision.

The fill-in questions page sets the two methods side by side, with typical timelines of one to four days for a partial fill-in and three to seven for a full removal.

Common Stockton questions

What is the difference between Stockton's Option A and Option B?

Option A leaves the shell for landscaping; Option B removes it for any future structure.

Can a Stockton homeowner pull the permit?

Yes for Option A, with an owner-builder form; Option B needs a licensed contractor.

How many holes does Stockton's Option A need?

At least four, each at least 1 ft across and 2 ft deep, with one at the deepest point.

What does a Stockton pool demolition permit cost?

Where can Stockton pool water be drained?
