A contractor's licence classes matter on a pool job. Santa Rosa permits are generally issued to holders of the A (general engineering), C-12 (earthwork and paving), C-21 (building moving and demolition) or C-53 (swimming pool) classes described in the state licence board's classification guide, and the accepted classes differ from city to city. Industry Bulletin 24-07 confirms that the $1,000 unlicensed-work exemption does not cover work that needs a permit, so a pool removal always needs a licensed contractor or an owner-builder permit.

Santa Rosa Pool Removal Permit Rules

Santa Rosa's Building Division issues a building permit for pool and spa removal, and Policy 2.5.30, effective 20 April 2016, sets out two options. The 2026 fee schedule lists minor demolition at $50.10 for plan check and $200.38 for inspection, and major demolition at $50.10 and $300.56; which category a pool falls under is not stated. Partial fill-in or full removal in Santa Rosa

Santa Rosa's Option A suits an area staying as landscape or open space: the shell stays below grade, at least three 1-ft holes go through the bottom, 15 inches of drain rock goes in, the top 2 feet of shell comes off, and the top 24 inches of fill is clean soil. The owner signs an acknowledgement to use the area as open space only and to disclose it on sale. Option B, for anything built or paved there, removes the whole shell with a geotechnical report and fill in 8-inch lifts compacted to at least 90%.

A partial fill-in breaks drainage holes through the floor, removes the top of the wall and fills the shell with soil. The method costs less and finishes faster, usually in one to four days, but most cities limit the area to landscaping afterwards. A full removal hauls away every piece of the shell and replaces it with fill compacted in layers to 90%, which takes three to seven days and is the route for anyone planning a structure over the old pool.

Published prices for Bay Area

Souza & Son's publishes $4,500 to $10,000 for a partial fill-in and $8,000 to $22,000 for a full removal of a 15 by 30 ft pool. Bye Bye Pool in Concord quotes $3,500 to $7,000 and $9,000 to $19,000, and Walberg, Inc. gives $5,000 to $12,000 and $12,000 to $30,000 for Northern California.

Four things move a quote more than anything else: equipment access (about $2,000 more where the side yard is under 6 ft wide), deck removal ($500 to $1,700), fill dirt (80 to 150 cubic yards for a full removal) and the engineer's compaction report ($1,500 to $4,000). The California pool removal cost guide sets out every published price by region and pool type.

Pool water has to go somewhere before demolition starts. The San Diego pools and spas fact sheet and LA Stormwater both allow the storm drain only for dechlorinated water within a set pH range, and Santa Rosa contractors generally follow the same practice. The Planning and Economic Development Department, Building Division confirms the local rule.

Removing a pool can lower the assessed value of a Santa Rosa property. The Board of Equalization's letter 86/09 treats removal of an improvement as a reason to reduce assessed value, but the reduction is tied to the pool's own assessed value, so an older pool may bring only a small change. Sellers should also expect the question on the state's seller disclosure form, which asks about fill on the property and about unpermitted work.
