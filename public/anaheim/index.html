Anaheim Pool Removal Permit Rules

Anaheim's Pool/Spa Permit Application B712 (August 2025) covers the construction, alteration, demolition and repair of a pool or spa, and its specification section has a demolition box for each. The form lists a $182 demolition fee that includes capping the utilities, alongside a $182 minimum permit fee. The city's Handout B804, dated September 2014, confirms that a permit is required before any structure is demolished. The Building Division is at 200 S. Anaheim Blvd., Suite 145, (714) 765-5153.

Partial fill-in or full removal in Anaheim

Anaheim publishes no rule on drainage holes, wall removal or compaction, so the Building Division decides what it accepts when the shell stays. The pool form adds two conditions that touch any job: excavated material may not be spread on the lot without city approval, and its second page is a stormwater self-exemption checklist that includes soil movement. Owners planning to build over the area should settle the fill standard with the Building Division first.

The fill-in questions page sets the two methods side by side, with typical timelines of one to four days for a partial fill-in and three to seven for a full removal.

Hauling the debris in Anaheim

Anaheim's construction and demolition page applies the 65% diversion target from state law and CALGreen and requires an application before a permit is issued. The city's 2026 information list names Republic Services as the franchise hauler: using its dumpster or transfer station meets the requirement with no further diversion reporting, while self-haulers keep receipts and file updated forms before final inspection. Republic's Anaheim flyer caps roll-off dumpsters at 10 tons and lists concrete, dirt and rock as clean debris.

Pool water has to go somewhere before demolition starts. Anaheim's municipal code section 6.56.080 makes it unlawful to drain a pool onto any public property other than into a sanitary sewer, or onto another person's property. The countywide H2OC pool brochure also asks for the sewer when possible and warns that some cities bar storm drain disposal, so the sewer cleanout is the route to plan on in Anaheim.

Sellers in Anaheim should keep the finaled permit, because the state's seller disclosure form asks about fill on the property and about unpermitted work.

Common Anaheim questions

Which permit covers pool removal in Anaheim?
The Swimming Pool/Spa Permit, which covers demolition as well as new pools.

What does an Anaheim pool demolition permit cost?
$182, including utility capping, on form B712 from August 2025.

Can Anaheim pool water go to the street?
No. City code allows draining onto public property only into a sanitary sewer.

Who hauls pool debris in Anaheim?
Republic Services, the franchise hauler; self-haulers keep receipts and show at least 65% recycled.
