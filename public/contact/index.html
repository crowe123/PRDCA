About This Site

Pool Removal California is an independent referral directory. The site is not a contractor and does not remove pools. Listed businesses may pay a referral fee when a homeowner is put in touch with them through the site, and that fee never changes the listing order: contractors with an active state licence appear first. The full referral disclosure and listing criteria explain how each business was checked.
