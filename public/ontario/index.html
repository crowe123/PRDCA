Ontario Pool Removal Permit Rules

Ontario's Building Department requires a building permit to demolish or remove an in-ground pool or spa, applied for through the Citizen Portal, which the city has required since July 2023. A homeowner, a B general contractor, a C-53 pool contractor or a C-21 demolition contractor may apply. Information Bulletin 111, effective 16 October 2024, asks for a site plan drawn to scale showing the pool's location, size and depth and its clearances from property lines, nearby structures and retaining walls. The permit fee follows the valuation table on the city's building fee page, with plan check at 80% of the permit fee and a minimum permit fee of $47.50.

Inspections and paperwork on an Ontario job

The plan goes through a concurrent review by Building, Planning, Engineering, Landscape, OMUC Environmental and Public Works, and a tracking number is assigned once the plan check fee is paid. A resubmittal waits until every department has finished its review.

Before the final inspection, Information Bulletin 111 requires:

  1. The shell removed completely from the site.
  2. All above-ground pool plumbing, including pumps and heaters, removed.
  3. The gas line disconnected and capped at the meter and at the equipment.
  4. The electrical subpanel and conductors removed back to the service supply.
  5. The fill placed as the plan shows.

The bulletin names one inspection, the final, which can be booked at (909) 395-2362 or online.

Partial fill-in or full removal in Ontario

Ontario allows one route: the whole shell leaves the site. The bulletin does not address leaving a shell in place, so a partial fill-in needs the Building Department's approval before anyone plans on it. The fill can be self-compacting material such as sand, or clean fill dirt compacted to at least 90% under the ASTM D1557 test, and the plan must show which.

The fill-in questions page sets the two methods side by side, with typical timelines of one to four days for a partial fill-in and three to seven for a full removal.

What a Ontario job costs

No contractor publishes Ontario prices. Because Ontario allows only full removal, the statewide full-removal range of about $8,000 to $30,000 is the one to plan around. The city's charges add the valuation-based permit and plan check, plus hauling: a 7-day bin at $95 and $369.29 for each inert or construction load on the July 2026 rate sheet.

The California pool removal cost guide covers what moves a quote, from side-yard access to fill dirt and the compaction report.

Hauling the debris in Ontario

An approved Construction and Demolition Recycling Plan is a condition of any building or demolition permit in Ontario. The plan commits the job to divert at least 65% of its debris and to use the City of Ontario hauling service, unless the contractor self-hauls in company-owned trucks and containers and keeps weight receipts, and it ends with a final compliance report to Integrated Waste. The program carries no extra fee. On the city's July 2026 rate sheet, a 7-day temporary bin rents for $95, an inert or construction load adds $369.29 for disposal, and loads over 12 tons pay $115 a ton.

Pool water has to go somewhere before demolition starts. Ontario's pool maintenance guidance lists three routes: reuse the water on landscaping, send it to the sewer between midnight and 6 am, or dechlorinate it to zero residual and wait 24 hours before draining to the street or storm drain. Water treated with metal-based algaecides such as copper sulfate goes to the sewer, and the city's stormwater rules ban chlorinated water and algae solids from the storm drain.

Sellers in Ontario should keep the finaled permit, because the state's seller disclosure form asks about fill on the property and about unpermitted work.

Common Ontario questions

Can a pool be filled in place in Ontario, California?
No. Information Bulletin 111 requires the entire shell removed; leaving it in place is not addressed.

What fill does Ontario accept?
Self-compacting fill such as sand, or clean fill dirt compacted to at least 90% under ASTM D1557.

How is an Ontario pool demolition permit priced?
By project valuation on the city's building fee table, with plan check at 80% of the permit fee and a $47.50 minimum.

Who hauls pool debris in Ontario?
The City of Ontario hauling service, unless the contractor self-hauls in its own trucks and containers and keeps weight receipts.

Is this Ontario, California or Ontario, Canada?
