A contractor's licence classes matter on a pool job. Irvine permits are generally issued to holders of the A (general engineering), C-12 (earthwork and paving), C-21 (building moving and demolition) or C-53 (swimming pool) classes described in the state licence board's classification guide, and the accepted classes differ from city to city. Industry Bulletin 24-07 confirms that the $1,000 unlicensed-work exemption does not cover work that needs a permit, so a pool removal always needs a licensed contractor or an owner-builder permit.

Irvine Pool Removal Permit Rules

Irvine's Building and Safety division issues a building permit at minimum fees, though the amount is not published. The city's pool removal page (Bulletin 217) requires the plans to state whether the fill is non-structural (landscaping) or structural (a building pad). Holes of at least 12 inches go through the bottom at six feet on centre, and a soils engineer supervises the backfill to 90% and supplies a final compaction report.

Partial fill-in or full removal in Irvine

Irvine asks the owner to choose at the plan stage: landscaping or building pad. A building pad needs full removal and engineer-supervised fill; landscaping allows a partial fill-in. Many Irvine homes sit under homeowners association rules, which may add their own approval step.

A partial fill-in breaks drainage holes through the floor, removes the top of the wall and fills the shell with soil. The method costs less and finishes faster, usually in one to four days, but most cities limit the area to landscaping afterwards. A full removal hauls away every piece of the shell and replaces it with fill compacted in layers to 90%, which takes three to seven days and is the route for anyone planning a structure over the old pool.

Published prices for Orange County

Frisch & Sons, an Orange County demolition contractor, publishes $12,000 to $20,000 for a partial fill-in and $18,000 to $30,000 or more for a full removal. Los Angeles firms start at $10,500 and $17,500 according to Los Angeles Pool Demolition Contractors, so Southern California prices begin well above the national cost guides.

Four things move a quote more than anything else: equipment access (about $2,000 more where the side yard is under 6 ft wide), deck removal ($500 to $1,700), fill dirt (80 to 150 cubic yards for a full removal) and the engineer's compaction report ($1,500 to $4,000). The California pool removal cost guide sets out every published price by region and pool type.

Pool water has to go somewhere before demolition starts. The San Diego pools and spas fact sheet and LA Stormwater both allow the storm drain only for dechlorinated water within a set pH range, and Irvine contractors generally follow the same practice. The Community Development Department, Building and Safety confirms the local rule.

Removing a pool can lower the assessed value of a Irvine property. The Board of Equalization's letter 86/09 treats removal of an improvement as a reason to reduce assessed value, but the reduction is tied to the pool's own assessed value, so an older pool may bring only a small change. Sellers should also expect the question on the state's seller disclosure form, which asks about fill on the property and about unpermitted work.
