Inspections on an Elk Grove job

For a pool abandoned in place, the holes are inspected before the pool is filled and the inspector is notified when the fill operation starts. For a full removal, the compaction report must be approved before the final. Construction over an abandoned pool stays barred unless the pool is re-abandoned to the full-removal standard.

Partial fill-in or full removal in Elk Grove

Full removal means taking out the entire pool and its utilities and backfilling under a qualified soils engineer to at least 90% relative density, with a compaction report approved before final; any alternative must come from the soils engineer and be approved before the permit. Abandoning in place needs a dimensioned plot plan, the bond beam, gunite and all improvements removed to at least 18 inches deep, two holes of at least 3 by 3 ft, and fill compacted in 6-inch layers under city inspection, with no soils engineer required. The city then places a building restriction on the property against construction in the pool area.

The fill-in questions page sets the two methods side by side, with typical timelines of one to four days for a partial fill-in and three to seven for a full removal.

Hauling the debris in Elk Grove

Elk Grove's construction and demolition recycling program covers all demolition projects, which pay a fee of $0.40 per $1,000 of value, at least $40 and at most $800, regardless of value. The job needs a Waste Management Plan and a Waste Log, and mixed loads go to a certified sorting facility. C&D hauling is open to the city's authorized haulers, about a dozen companies including Republic Services.

Pool water has to go somewhere before demolition starts. Elk Grove's pool draining handout makes it illegal to send chlorinated pool water to the storm drain, and allows the storm drain only below 0.01 parts per million of chlorine at a pH of 6.5 to 8.5 and not heated; saltwater pools go to the sewer only. SacSewer allows periodic pool discharges to the sewer through a cleanout without advance permission or a permit, and the handout warns never to open a manhole.

Sellers in Elk Grove should keep the finaled permit, because the state's seller disclosure form asks about fill on the property and about unpermitted work.

Common Elk Grove questions

What does an Elk Grove pool demolition permit cost?
$85, including utility capping, on the 2026 fee booklet, plus a $250 encroachment permit where needed.

Can a pool be abandoned in place in Elk Grove?
Yes, with the top 18 inches removed, two 3 by 3 ft holes and 6-inch compacted layers, followed by a building restriction on the area.

Does an Elk Grove full removal need a soils engineer?
Yes. The backfill is placed under a soils engineer to at least 90%, with a compaction report before final.

Can Elk Grove pool water go down the sewer?
Yes. SacSewer allows periodic pool discharges through a cleanout without a permit.
