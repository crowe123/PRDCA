A contractor's licence classes matter on a pool job. Fontana permits are generally issued to holders of the A (general engineering), C-12 (earthwork and paving), C-21 (building moving and demolition) or C-53 (swimming pool) classes described in the state licence board's classification guide, and the accepted classes differ from city to city. Industry Bulletin 24-07 confirms that the $1,000 unlicensed-work exemption does not cover work that needs a permit, so a pool removal always needs a licensed contractor or an owner-builder permit.

Fontana Pool Removal Permit Rules

Fontana's Department of Building and Safety issues a demolition permit, priced at $120 on the 2023 schedule, which needs a recheck. The city's pool demolition handout (2026) requires six 1-ft openings in the bottom and the top two feet of wall removed when the shell stays. The handout also states directly that building over the area needs the shell removed and the fill compacted and certified.

Partial fill-in or full removal in Fontana

Fontana's handout says plainly what most cities imply: a structure may go over a former pool only where the shell was removed and the fill compacted to 90% and certified by an engineer. A partial fill-in with six openings suits a yard that stays as landscape.

A partial fill-in breaks drainage holes through the floor, removes the top of the wall and fills the shell with soil. The method costs less and finishes faster, usually in one to four days, but most cities limit the area to landscaping afterwards. A full removal hauls away every piece of the shell and replaces it with fill compacted in layers to 90%, which takes three to seven days and is the route for anyone planning a structure over the old pool.

Published prices for Inland Empire

No contractor in this part of California publishes local prices, so the statewide figures apply. Northern California firms such as Walberg, Inc. quote $3,500 to $12,000 for a partial fill-in and $8,000 to $30,000 for a full removal, and Los Angeles firms start at $10,500 and $17,500. National guides such as HomeAdvisor run lower and work best as a floor.

Four things move a quote more than anything else: equipment access (about $2,000 more where the side yard is under 6 ft wide), deck removal ($500 to $1,700), fill dirt (80 to 150 cubic yards for a full removal) and the engineer's compaction report ($1,500 to $4,000). The California pool removal cost guide sets out every published price by region and pool type.

Pool water has to go somewhere before demolition starts. The San Diego pools and spas fact sheet and LA Stormwater both allow the storm drain only for dechlorinated water within a set pH range, and Fontana contractors generally follow the same practice. The Department of Building and Safety confirms the local rule.

Removing a pool can lower the assessed value of a Fontana property. The Board of Equalization's letter 86/09 treats removal of an improvement as a reason to reduce assessed value, but the reduction is tied to the pool's own assessed value, so an older pool may bring only a small change. Sellers should also expect the question on the state's seller disclosure form, which asks about fill on the property and about unpermitted work.
